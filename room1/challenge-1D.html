5-PS1-1 Particles Too Small to See

5-PS1-1 · Energy and Matter

Challenge 4 of 4

Read the lab notes. Maya and Jordan shrink into the Haunted Particle Lab. A purple potion looks empty after they stir in sugar. Jordan says the sugar vanished. Maya says the sugar is still there as particles too small to see. They taste a drop. It is still sweet. Later they leave the potion open. Water particles leave into the air. Tiny sugar crystals appear on the glass. The crystals were not new matter from nowhere. They were sugar particles that stayed behind when water particles moved away. The friends write a model: matter is made of particles too small to see. Mixing and drying change how those particles are arranged, but the particles do not disappear.
